Subject: A little project idea for Pasong's — from Alex, Alex, and me

Hi [Owner's name],

I'm Kegan — I've stopped by Pasong's a few times and always leave full and happy.
A couple of my friends who eat there a lot, Alex Mixter and Alex Larson, mentioned
you might be someone worth pitching this to.

Here's the idea: a bunch of us have shown up when the posted hours online didn't
quite match reality — closed a little early, or open when a listing said you
weren't. Alex and Alex and I got to talking and thought: what if there were a
dead-simple website — just "Is Pasong's open right now?" — that actually reflects
what's happening at the register, not just a static hours listing someone forgot
to update?

The idea is: your point-of-sale system (whatever you use — Square, Clover, Toast,
etc.) already knows in real time whether someone's working the register. I'd build
a tiny webpage that checks that — with the absolute minimum access needed. No
access to payments, sales totals, menu, pricing, refunds, or customer data — just
a single read-only permission to see "is a shift open / has a sale happened
recently." You'd grant it yourself with one click from your own POS dashboard, and
you could revoke it just as easily any time you wanted.

I'd do the whole thing for free — no strings, no ask for anything in return. We
just love the restaurant and thought it'd be a nice, low-effort win for people
trying to time a visit.

If you're open to it, the only thing I'd need from you is:
1. Which POS system you use, and
2. A few minutes to walk through the one-click, read-only permission grant.

No pressure at all if it's not your thing — just wanted to put the idea in front
of you since it came from people who already love eating there.

Thanks for everything you make,
